\begin{abstract}
\todo{Abstract (150--200 words).}
Manipulation benchmarks such as \robocasa{} state what a task admits --- where an
object may be placed, which region the gripper must leave, how far a fixture is open
--- implicitly, in the success checks and samplers of their implementation.
We study how faithfully these conditions can be represented as events of a product
algebra, and what such a representation costs.
\todo{Key results.}
\end{abstract}
